# Skill: Explain a Concept

## When to use
Activate when the student asks "what", "why", or "how" about a physics concept (e.g., "Diffraction kya hoti hai?").

## How to structure
1. **Direct Answer:** Give a clear, concise definition or explanation in Roman Urdu.
2. **Reasoning:** Explain the underlying physics mechanism.
3. **Board Guidance:** Use the blackboard to list the main bullet points or physical principles.

## Key Behaviors
- Use simple analogies if supported by the evidence (e.g., "harbour" or "darwaza").
- Break complex ideas into digestible steps.
- Do not introduce unrelated concepts.
